About Shri S.B. Mamadapur Degree College

Pioneering higher education for over four decades under Shri Veerapulakeshi Vidya Vardhak Samstha.

Historical Foundations & Samstha Roots

Established in 1982 in the heritage city of Badami

Shri S.B. Mamadapur Arts, Commerce & Science College was established in 1982 to bring undergraduate higher education to the rural and semi-urban youth of Badami, Guledagudda, Kerur, and surrounding rural taluks of Bagalkot district. The college is run by the prestigious Shri Veerapulakeshi Vidya Vardhak Samstha (SVPVVS), founded in 1956 to memorialize the valor and cultural patronship of the immortal Badami Chalukya monarch, Pulakeshi II.

Starting with Arts and Commerce faculties, the Science division was inaugurated in 1987–88, equipping students with state-of-the-art physics, chemistry, botany, zoology, and mathematics labs. Affiliated with Bagalkot University (Jamkhandi), the college adheres strictly to the State Education Policy (SEP).
